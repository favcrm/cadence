# Quotation authoring and reconciliation

Record each selected item's quantity/unit, price, currency, dated approved source, scope and option status. Establish tax basis, discount order, precision, rounding and validity/terms from the governing commercial inputs. Keep internal costs separate from customer prices; disclose unit rates where required by the client form.

Use existing trusted calculation facilities (for example the native draft model where its contract fits, or an inspected Decimal calculation for a local agreed draft). Do not create a calculator just to populate this skill. Record exact inputs, formulas, rounding and results. Independently reconcile the output using a different calculation representation or reviewer; repeating the same calculator result is insufficient.

Exclude unselected alternatives from the selected base and label them separately. A required item without a price blocks the full total and final-price/issuable claim; show any known-items total explicitly as partial. Never drop the mandatory item or price it at zero to pass validation. Native line items have no selection or missing-price flag: filter options explicitly and do not misrepresent a partial native calculation as a complete quotation.

Check line extensions, selected subtotal, discount, tax basis/rate and total, plus cross-document quantities and scope. A change in option selection or price returns to Stage 1; layout repairs do not. Label a draft `SUGGESTED — NOT APPROVED — HUMAN REVIEW REQUIRED`, with unresolved decisions visible. Request confirmation does not grant issuance, numbering, signing or sending authority.
