# App template

This is the starter screen for a Cadence app. Replace the identity, summary,
surface and workflow with the app you are building.

The host owns the frame, the board-wide drawer, its scrim and the close
affordance. This app owns the surface and uses `@cadence/app-kit` for the
private screen bridge.
